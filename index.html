🎁 Secret Santa

Draw names. Keep the secret.

A free Secret Santa app for Android. No email, no signup — and the organizer never sees who got whom.

Get it on Google Play

Free, supported by ads. Works in English, Português and Español.

How it works

01

Add everyone

Type names or paste the list straight from your group chat. Add gift ideas and a budget if you like.

02

Set the rules

Couples and siblings who shouldn't draw each other? Mark them, and the draw respects it.

03

Reveal

Send each person their own private link, or pass the phone around the room.

Why people switch to it

Nothing to sign up for

No emails that land in spam, no accounts, no paywall to send the results.

The organizer plays too

Results travel inside each person's link. The organizer's screen only shows who has been sent theirs.

A draw that adds up

Everyone gives exactly one gift and gets exactly one. Nobody draws themselves, nobody is left out.
